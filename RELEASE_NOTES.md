PathRenamer 1.5.0 can do the work for you.

- **Watch folder**: point PathRenamer at your Downloads folder (Tools → Watch Folder). New movies and episodes are matched as soon as they finish downloading, and the ones it's sure about can be renamed into your library on their own.
- **Plex**: sign in with Plex (Settings → Automation) and Plex scans the new files right after renaming, so they show up in seconds.
- **Subtitles**: download subtitles from OpenSubtitles.com for any file in the preview (Tools → Download Subtitles), or have missing languages downloaded after every rename.
- **Better names for files without tags**: resolution and codecs are read from the video itself when the name doesn't say, e.g. "Home Video (2019) 1080p H.264".

Renaming by hand stays free. The automatic parts are part of PathRenamer Pro; during early access every feature is unlocked.

Install with **PathRenamer-Setup-1.5.0.exe**. It upgrades an existing installation and keeps your settings. **PathRenamer.exe** is the same app without an installer.
