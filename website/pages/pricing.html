Questions about Pro

What happens when the trial ends?

The automatic features switch off, and everything you do by hand keeps working. Nothing is renamed or removed. Turn them back on any time with a Pro key.

How do I start the trial?

Turn on any Pro feature in Settings → Automation or Settings → Subtitles. The {{ trial_days }}-day trial starts then. No account or card needed.

Does Pro need the internet all the time?

No. PathRenamer checks your key once a week and keeps Pro working for 30 days without a connection.

Can I move my license to a new PC?

Yes. In Settings → Pro, click Remove From This PC on the old computer, then activate the key on the new one.

Who handles payments?

Polar, which takes care of payment, receipts, and sales tax or VAT where you live. PathRenamer never sees your card.
