Privacy

PathRenamer works on your computer. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never uploads your videos. Here is everything it sends, and where.

The app

ServiceWhenWhat it gets
TVmazeMatching TV shows and animeThe show name; season and episode numbers
Cinemeta (Stremio)Matching moviesThe movie title
AniDBMatching animeA download of its title list; anime ids for episode names
TMDBOnly if you add your own TMDB keyMovie and show titles, with your key
OpenSubtitles.comOnly when subtitles are downloadedThe title, season and episode, your languages, the video's OpenSubtitles fingerprint (a number made from its size and its first and last 64 KB), and your username and password if you entered them
Your Plex serverOnly if you connect PlexRequests to scan the folders that changed
plex.tvOnly when you click Sign in with PlexA sign-in code; you sign in on plex.tv itself
PolarOnly for Pro license keysYour license key and your computer's name (so you can tell your computers apart when moving a license)

Your settings, rename history and logs stay in %APPDATA%\PathRenamer on your computer. The Plex token and OpenSubtitles password are stored encrypted for your Windows account.

Buying Pro

Payments are handled by Polar as the seller of record: Polar collects your name, email and payment details to sell you the license and send your key, under Polar's privacy policy. PathRenamer never sees your card.

This website

This site uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub privacy statement. Downloads come from GitHub Releases.
